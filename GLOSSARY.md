# Dictionarium Latinum

A Latin–English dictionary for learners, filed by hand by one editor, with the grammar tables its entries link into.

## Language

### The dictionary

**Lemma**:
The canonical form a word is filed under; one entry per lemma (and homonym).
_Avoid_: headword, word (when the filed form is meant)

**Sense**:
One meaning of an entry, ranked by how often it is used; position is the rank.
_Avoid_: definition, meaning

**Homonym**:
A separate entry that shares its lemma's spelling, addressed by number.

**Filing**:
The editor reading, checking and submitting an entry; nothing is filed without a person's review.
_Avoid_: import, adding

### Frequency

**Core**:
The words LASLA ranks 1–1,000 by how often they appear in classical Latin.
_Avoid_: basic vocabulary, top 1000

**Common**:
The words LASLA ranks 1,001–3,000.

### Milestones

**v1**:
Every Core word is filed. Due by 31 March 2027.

**v2**:
2,000 entries are filed in total, Core or not.

**v3**:
Every Core and every Common word is filed.
_Avoid_: launch, release (for these milestones)

**Weekly target**:
The number of Core words to file each week to reach v1 by its deadline: 50.

**Reading list**:
An editor-only list of words from the books the editor reads, each with its source and the lesson that introduces it; it decides which words the desk suggests next.
_Avoid_: textbook list, vocabulary list

**Behind**:
The state when the projected v1 date, at the recent filing pace, falls after the deadline.
_Avoid_: late, off track
